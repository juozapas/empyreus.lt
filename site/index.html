Services

Strategy, people and software

Start with a decision to make, a team to prepare or a system to build. We take a focused brief, or work across all three.

  1. 01

    Fractional CTO

    Senior technology leadership, part-time. We help founders and executives set technical priorities, review architecture, make hiring and supplier decisions, and keep delivery aligned with the business.

    Meet the CTO on LinkedIn

  2. 02

    AI strategy and consultancy

    Set priorities for AI investment. We assess use cases, data and feasibility, compare building with buying, and define a roadmap with costs, responsibilities and measures of success.

  3. 03

    AI fluency programmes

    Practical programmes for leaders and teams, built around their own work: how to use AI tools well, assess the output and recognise the limits, so the skills hold up long after the sessions end.

  4. 04

    Organisational transformation

    Workflows, responsibilities and decision points redesigned around AI. We work with your people to decide what to automate, where human review belongs, and how to measure whether the new way of working is better.

  5. 05

    AI products and agents

    Assistants, agents and document-processing tools built around a defined task, connected to your data and systems, tested on real tasks, with clear limits on what runs without human review.

  6. 06

    Software engineering

    Web, mobile, backend and cloud software designed, built and maintained by senior engineers: new products, extensions to existing systems, and the infrastructure AI needs to work in your business.

Approach

From decision to daily use

We agree what needs to change, test it in real work, and prepare your team to own it.

  1. 01

    Define the priority

    We assess the problem, your data and current ways of working, then choose together what to tackle first and agree scope, estimated cost, ownership and measures of success.

  2. 02

    Build and test

    Practical sessions, pilots or working software, whichever the priority calls for. Your team uses the results, gives feedback and judges progress against the agreed measures before wider adoption.

  3. 03

    Put it into practice

    We support the rollout and measure results against the agreed goals. You get practical guidance, clear responsibility for ongoing decisions, the code for any software we build, and an agreed plan for further support.
